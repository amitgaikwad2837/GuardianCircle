What it does

Safety features built for real life

From one-tap SOS to automatic crash detection — every feature works locally, with no backend required.

🆘

One-tap SOS

Instantly alert your trusted guardians via encrypted FCM and fallback SMS. Works even without internet.

🛡️

Guardian network

Add up to 5 trusted contacts. Pair securely via QR code with end-to-end encrypted notifications.

🚗

Vehicle crash detection

Accelerometer-based crash detection triggers an automatic SOS countdown if you don't respond.

🧍

Fall detection

Detects sudden falls and gives you a configurable window to cancel before alerting your guardians.

📍

Journey mode

Share a live trip with check-in intervals. If you miss a check-in, guardians are automatically notified.

🔒

Duress PIN

Enter a secret duress PIN to silently trigger SOS while appearing to unlock the app normally.

🎭

Decoy mode

The app can disguise itself as a different app icon and name to avoid detection in unsafe situations.

🤖

AI safety assistant

Bring your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini). Conversations never leave your device.

How it works

Set up in under 5 minutes

No sign-up, no email verification, no waiting.

1

Download and open

Install from Google Play. The app generates a private cryptographic identity on your device — nothing is sent to any server.

2

Add a guardian

Share a QR code with a trusted person. They scan it to pair — keys are exchanged end-to-end with fingerprint verification to prevent MITM attacks.

3

Grant permissions

Location, SMS, and notification permissions are explained with plain language before you grant them. You can use the app with any subset of permissions.

4

You're protected

Tap the SOS button, trigger a journey, or let crash/fall detection run quietly in the background. Your guardians are alerted the moment you need help.
